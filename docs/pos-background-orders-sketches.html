Background order status language

The stack scrolls sideways without a scrollbar. Color and shape identify each state; the full list and payment screen retain status words.

UnconfirmedPayment seen; no wedge yet
Confirming2 of 10 shown = 20% wedge

PaidOne full coin · settled

!Double spendUrgent payment warning
