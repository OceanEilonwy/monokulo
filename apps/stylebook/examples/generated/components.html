Once its island attaches, a Copy button copies the whole value (or, where the clipboard is refused, selects it to copy by hand), and a shortened form can stand in for it on screen.

Props
PropTypeAbout
valueStringRequired. The whole value: what is copied.
copy_labelStringThe Copy button's accessible name: what it copies. Default: "Copy full value".
labelled_byOption<String>The id of what names the value, when something on the page does.
truncateTruncate (end | middle | segments)How a long value is shortened on screen. The whole value is always what is copied, and what focus reveals. Default: end.
headnumberMiddle's characters kept at the start. Default: 8.
tailnumberMiddle's characters kept at the end. Default: 8.
segmentsOption<String>Segments' ranges.

GroupedValue

A long value shown whole, for someone to check by eye against the copy they paste: in groups of a few characters, alternately strong and muted, the first and last group marked, and a Copy button under it. An address to pay; a key's fingerprint. Build it with [GroupedValue::of], which splits the value. A screen reader hears the value whole, never group by group. Without JavaScript a tap selects all of it; once its island attaches, the button copies it and says so, and the value stays on screen as it was.

Props
PropTypeAbout
valueStringRequired. The whole value: what is copied, and what a screen reader hears.

copy_labelStringThe Copy button's words: what it copies ("Copy address"). Default: "Copy".
hintOption<Slot>Under it: what to check ("Check your wallet shows 8FASi4 at the start and NoVKf at the end").
voidboolShown, but not to be used any more (an address an order no longer takes payments at): struck through, faded, nothing to copy it with. Default: false.
restAttrs

CopyBlock

Lines to copy whole: a command, a config file, a key. Without JavaScript it is a pre to select. Once its island attaches, a Copy button over its top right copies it; with comment ("#"), a note after that marker is left out (the marker starting a line or after a space: # inside a word stays).

The page's settings form: one per page. label names what has unsaved changes when leaving is asked about ("Abuse protection has unsaved changes"). With fixi_target, a save swaps its answer into that element without reloading; without, it's a plain POST and the page it redirects to.

SaveBar

The page's one save bar, along the bottom of the window: message, then Discard changes (a link to the page as saved) and Save. Without JavaScript it's always there; with it, only while something changed, a save was refused, or leaving is asked about, and it names the cards changed. Red after a save that refused something.

PageHeader

The title is required, so no page goes without one. Beside the title: a status or a badge (aside), and at its right the page's own actions.

StatusDot

A glowing dot linking to the status page (in the site's bar and the POS's alike), as the instance was when the page was rendered, so it is right without JavaScript. Once its island attaches it polls poll every 30 seconds while the tab is in sight. Its title and accessible name carry the words.
